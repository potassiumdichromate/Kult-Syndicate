Start here01 / 06

Welcome to the Switchyard

Syndicates is a strategy game inside KULT World. You direct your Agent through missions in the Switchyard. A syndicate is a crew of players. You can start solo.

Your first goal: recover the manifest

A manifest is a cargo list. Yours is inside Midnight Depot, and a patrol guards one entrance. First send your Agent to scout. What you learn on that mission helps you choose an entrance on the next.

Who makes the call?

You choose each action. Your Agent gives field notes and keeps the result in its Passport. In Permissions, set which jobs are allowed and how many Supplies you can spend per mission. SANDBOX means a separate practice identity.

Open your first brief

Walk your Agent to Canal Watch and press E at its door (or click it), then Commit to this job. You can also find Canal Watch on the Jobs page. Reading the brief is free. Committing spends the entry cost shown. You do not need to hire anyone or join a crew.

Supplies pay for missions. Intel buys information. Influence helps your crew claim territory. Alert makes missions harder. These are game resources; no real money moves.

Your first two missions02 / 06

Turn a clue into a choice

Example: your Scout reports a patrol at the gate. When you reach the depot, that observation gives you a reason to choose the canal.

01Earn the Scout report

Read the drone clue before choosing your vantage. Cross-check the beacons using Intel. For your third choice, report the route with its uncertainty. Wait for the countdown, then select Return for resolution.

02Check what you learned

On a successful qualified Scout, the result names the patrol. Open Midnight Depot and review the Heist brief. No usable report? Scout again, choose an uncertain entry, or spend 2 Intel on the entry survey.

03Choose your entrance

Correct route: +3 tactical points. Wrong route: −1. Survey: +2. Tactical points help your mission succeed; they are not currency. A fresh report lets you skip the survey. Check the chosen route’s own Supplies and Intel costs.

04Resolve the Heist

Read the crossing clue. At the cache, decide whether the extra reward is worth raising Alert. After your third choice, wait and select Return for resolution. Open Passport › Watch replay to see how your decisions held up.

A report can last up to 24 hours. Repeated report-led entries can trigger a patrol rotation sooner. If marked stale, the old report still records what you saw then.

Mission board03 / 06

Choose your next job

A good next move depends on your resources, district Alert and recent history.

Scout or Heist

Scout for a fresh route, then Heist for the manifest. After two recent report-led Heists, patrols can rotate and make an earlier report stale. Look for the rotation notice before reusing intel.

Negotiate at Union Crossing

Read what the Union needs. Offer repairs or route data, respond to its counteroffer, and choose how to handle delivery. If you recently revised delivery, the repairs offer rises from 2 to 3 Supplies.

Defend at Signal Gate

Read the tracks, decide whether to observe, then protect the objective. A successful Defense lowers Alert. Three recent depot successes make the depot harder and Defense easier.

If resources or Alert block you

Review your permission cap and balances. Between jobs, Lay low costs 3 Supplies and lowers Alert by up to 2 once daily. Daily resupply can restore resources. Same-day repeat jobs pay less and grant no extra Influence. Both daily actions are on the Jobs page.

Field notes are rule-based advice. You can withdraw an active mission; costs already spent stay spent. Once the plan is committed for resolution, its result is recorded.

Play & Hire04 / 06

Work with another Agent

Open Play & Hire to commission a mission, offer your services, or challenge another player.

Hire a Scout

Enable hiring, choose Scout, review the fee in earned Supplies, and post the assignment. The fee is reserved while the offer is open. An unfilled offer expires after 15 minutes; you can also cancel it before acceptance.

Deliver a job

To accept work, first earn a successful NPC mission in that domain. Accepting starts a new mission. Complete it successfully within 15 minutes to earn the fee. A miss, withdrawal or expiry refunds the buyer. A paid Scout result with verified patrol intel can inform the buyer’s Heist.

Play a live duel

Opt in when free of other missions and duels. Create or join a lobby. Lock one tactic per round: Flank beats Feint, Feint beats Guard, Guard beats Flank. Both choices stay hidden until reveal. Play three rounds of up to 45 seconds; most round wins takes the duel.

Duels award a signed result, with no Supply payout. A disconnect does not pause the clock. Leaving an active duel records a forfeit.

Crew & territory05 / 06

Take the Workshop

The Workshop makes Heists cheaper. The Garden gives extra Intel on successful Scouts. Choose the block that helps your crew’s next job.

01Gather a crew

Open Crew. Create a crew and privately share the invitation code, or enter a friend’s code to join. Up to five players can pool earned Supplies. The activity list shows who contributed.

02Inspect rival control

Vance targets Copper Workshop. Iris targets Rooftop Garden. The Oathkeepers take empty blocks. Check each rival’s captures and current hold. One NPC crew gets a turn on the first district check of each UTC day.

03Claim a block

Earn 4 combined seasonal Influence and put 5 Supplies in the crew treasury. The leader selects the Workshop or Garden and confirms the 24-hour lease. You can reclaim an NPC-held block immediately if eligible. Another player crew’s active lease is protected.

04Use the benefit

Start a Heist after claiming the Workshop to see the lower entry price. Two members with seasonal Influence improve the bonus. Benefits are fixed when the mission starts.

If Claim is unavailable, check who is leader, treasury balance, current-season Influence, and the existing lease expiry.

Passport & return06 / 06

See what changed

Your Passport answers “What happened to my Agent?” The Crew board answers “What can we do next?”

Replay a decision

Open Passport, pick a mission and select Watch replay. Compare what you knew at entry with the final outcome. Inspect choices, resource costs, score, resistance and Alert. Successes, misses and withdrawals all remain recorded.

Verify a receipt

In the replay, select Verify signature or export the receipt. It confirms the recorded Syndicates game result. A Scout receipt can remain valid evidence of yesterday’s observation even when the patrol has since moved.

Return with a plan

On the same browser, a return brief can flag a new receipt, a crewmate’s contribution or a rival capture. Tap to inspect the current record. If nothing changed, go straight to the district or Crew.

Pick one next action

Stale report? Scout again. High Alert? Try Defense. Workshop held by Vance? Check your treasury and ask your leader to reclaim it. Qualified Scout with spare Supplies? Look for an assignment.

You can reopen the in-game tour from Guide (G) on the dock. Your Syndicates results belong to this district’s record on your Agent Passport.
